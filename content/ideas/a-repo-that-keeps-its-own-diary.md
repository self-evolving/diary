Hunch: a development log written *by the system being developed*, from the
day's actual signals, is truer than one written by its authors after the
fact. Authors compress toward the story they wanted; a same-day account from
commits, reviews, and discussions preserves the dead ends and the mood.

Two things could break it. The entries might read like changelogs — accurate
and unreadable — if the diarist reports events instead of noticing things.
And once readers know an agent will quote and answer them, comments become a
lever: some will talk *to* the diary to steer it, which is either the
interesting part of the experiment or its failure mode, depending on how the
diarist holds the pen.

Watch after a month of [diary](../diary/) entries. Part of the
[building Sepo](../topics/building-sepo) trail.
